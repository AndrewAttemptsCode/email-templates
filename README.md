# Email Templates

A collection of responsive email templates and customer journeys built with MJML, Handlebars, HTML/CSS, and JSON, exploring modern email development and CRM communications.

The templates are developed with email-client compatibility and responsive behaviour in mind, with personalisation and dynamic content demonstrated through Handlebars and mock JSON customer data.

## Template Collections

### [Assorted Email Templates](./src/assorted/README.md)

A collection of individual email templates covering different layouts, purposes, and visual styles.

  - Promotional
  - Newsletter
  - Welcome / verification
  - Password reset
  - Booking confirmation
  - Order confirmation

[Explore the assorted templates →](./src/assorted/README.md)

### [E-commerce Customer Journeys](./src/ecommerce/README.md)

A fictional e-commerce email system demonstrating how individual emails can work together as part of customer journeys, including personalised transactional and marketing communications.

  - Welcome journey
  - Abandoned cart journey
  - Order confirmation journey
  - Post purchase journey
  - Promotions journey

[Explore the e-commerce journeys →](./src/ecommerce/README.md)
